# Bloc Party — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
General neighbors of all ages — including older homeowners and young college renters —
borrowing and lending everyday items casually. Used on phones, mostly during the day,
in a relaxed "I need a ladder this weekend" mindset. Not power users; legibility and
obvious affordances matter more than density or delight-through-novelty.

### Brand Personality
Warm, trustworthy, neighborly. Feels like borrowing a ladder from the folks next door —
a friendly front-porch handshake, not a marketplace transaction and not a civic social feed.

Three words: **warm, trustworthy, neighborly.**

### Aesthetic Direction — "Front Porch"
- Light-first theme (existing light/dark toggle preserved).
- Warm paper-cream surfaces (`#FBF7EF` family), tinted neutrals toward the brand hue — never pure white/black.
- **Leaf green** primary (~`#2E7D4F` family) + **terracotta** accent (~`#B85C38` family).
- Bricolage Grotesque for display/headers, Gabarito for body — bundled locally, no runtime fetching.
- Real item photos are the hero; UI chrome recedes (hairline borders, 16px radii, low elevation).
- Generous touch targets (min 48px), body text ≥ 15px for all-ages legibility.
- Anti-references: NOT Nextdoor (cluttered civic feed), NOT Facebook Marketplace (dense gray card grid).

### Design Principles
1. Legibility first for all ages — big type, clear labels, obvious states.
2. Photos are the hero — chrome gets out of the way.
3. Every control looks made by the same hand — one radius, one scale, one palette.
4. Empty states teach the interface; nothing is just "nothing here".
5. Warmth comes from palette and copy, not from decoration or gradients.
